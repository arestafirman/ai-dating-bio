## The bio

> Looking for someone who texts back in under a second, because I have timed your replies and the average is 47 minutes, and I have already forgiven you twice.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the bio — one line, 157 characters, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`, exactly as the competition gate requires (UTF-8, 1–180 characters, single line).

```sh
node main.ts
```
